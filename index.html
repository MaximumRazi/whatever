Scattering by wavelength

Short wavelengths (blue) scatter more strongly than long wavelengths (red). When sunlight travels through more air at sunset, blues are scattered out of the direct path, making the sun and nearby sky look redder.

Quick compare: Earth vs Mars vs Venus

Planet Main atmosphere Typical daytime sky Sunset tendency
Earth N₂ + O₂, moderate aerosols Blue Red/orange sunsets
Mars Thin CO₂ + dust Butterscotch/tan Bluish sunsets near the sun (dust forward-scattering)
Venus Very thick CO₂ + sulfuric acid clouds Yellowish diffuse glow (surface receives very little direct blue sky) Muted and highly cloud-filtered
